Web pages too.

Open a URL instead of launching an app. reel drives Chrome, Brave, Edge or Safari's WebKit itself, records the page with its sound, and leaves your mouse and keyboard alone. Here it plays Satie's first Gymnopédie on Wikimedia Commons, recorded by Musopen and in the public domain. Web pages

Code too.

Screen code walks through source in an editor of reel's own: the file and the lines that matter, a focus that dims the rest, numbered marks, callouts, typing at your pace, and files side by side with the relations between them drawn in. Nothing to install, no window, no permissions, and the same take every time. Code

The command line too.

Screen terminal runs a real shell in a terminal of reel's own: it waits on what commands print, covers secrets as they're typed, and points at the output that matters. Here reel checks and records a reel of its own. No permissions needed. Terminal

Record once. Set Profiles renders a cut for each place it's going, each in its shape and under its limits.

  • github

    READMEs, issues and pull requests: 1920 wide, under GitHub's 10 MB, with a GIF.

  • web

    Your landing page and docs: 1920 wide, light enough to autoplay.

  • social

    YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Instagram Reels: 1080×1920, subtitled for feeds that play muted.

  • feed

    X, Bluesky, Mastodon and LinkedIn: 1920×1080, under their size and length limits.

  • square

    Instagram and LinkedIn posts: 1080×1080.

  • highest

    Lossless ProRes, to edit further.

Install

Requires macOS 15 or later, Apple silicon. Then reel doctor, and your first reel.

FAQ

Why does reel ask for Screen Recording?

macOS requires it for any program that captures the screen. reel records only the window, or the page, a reel points it at, with its sound.

Why Accessibility?

It lets reel click, type and press keys in the app on your behalf. Both permissions are granted in System Settings, Privacy and Security.

Which Macs are supported?

macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.

Does it record web pages?

Yes: Open a URL instead of launching an app. WebKit is built in, or Set Browser chromium. A web reel leaves your mouse and keyboard alone.

Can I skip the setup, like a cookie banner or getting to the right screen?

Yes: whatever happens between Hide and Show is left out of every video. Hide, click the banner away, open the right view, Show, and the video starts there.

Can I change a caption without recording again?

Yes. Captions, zooms, speed-ups, cuts, title cards and every output setting are applied when rendering: reel render. Only a change to what happens in the app needs a new recording.
